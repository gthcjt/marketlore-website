MarketLore may also collect limited technical and operational information generated through use of its services, such as authentication activity, service requests, error information, and security or operational logs.

How We Use Information

MarketLore uses information to provide, operate, secure, maintain, and support its products and services. This may include:

  • Establishing and administering customer access;
  • Authenticating users and validating subscriptions or entitlements;
  • Providing requested products, services, and support;
  • Maintaining service reliability, security, and performance;
  • Investigating errors, misuse, or security events;
  • Communicating with customers regarding their service; and
  • Complying with applicable legal and contractual obligations.

MarketLore does not sell personal information.

MarketLore products and services may be made available through third-party marketplaces, distribution platforms, technology providers, or other service providers.

When a customer obtains MarketLore services through such a platform, MarketLore may receive information from that platform that is necessary to identify the customer, validate the applicable subscription or entitlement, provision access, and administer the service.

Data Storage and Security

MarketLore uses cloud-based infrastructure and service providers to operate and deliver its products and services.

MarketLore employs technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Data Sharing

MarketLore does not sell customer information or personal information.

MarketLore may share information with service providers, infrastructure providers, marketplaces, distribution platforms, or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, or deliver MarketLore services.

MarketLore may also disclose information when required by applicable law or legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or integrity of MarketLore, its customers, its services, or others.

Data Retention and Deletion

MarketLore retains customer, technical, and service information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide and administer its services, maintain security and operational records, comply with applicable legal or contractual obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.
